Hi! My name is Julia, I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Heuristics, Analysis and Learning Laboratory at the [Universidade Federal do ABC](https://www.ufabc.edu.br/), Santo André, SP, Brazil, supervised by [Prof. Fabrício Olivetti de França](https://folivetti.github.io/). 

My research is centered around Symbolic Regression (SR) for Equation Discovery, combining various techniques from Scientific Machine Learning and Statistics. 
Currently, I am investigating Bayesian and frequentist methods for Uncertainty Quantification and their applicability to SR. 
My goal is to develop methods that can account for uncertainty in equation discovery, particularly in challenging settings such as noisy or limited data, and as such provide more reliable symbolic models for scientific applications.
For my postdoctoral studies, I was awarded a scholarship from [FAPESP](https://fapesp.br/).
